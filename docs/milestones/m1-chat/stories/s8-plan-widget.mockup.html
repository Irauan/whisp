8. Loading and error of the widget

Neither applies. The widget has no load and no command of its own. It shows the latest plan entry of the snapshot of sessions.open, and each event updates it at once. The waiting state is the step in progress with its spinner (state 2). With reduced motion, the spinner stops, like the other spinners of the app.
